About StudentHub Portal

Empowering students and faculty with a unified platform for seamless academic tracking, course management, attendance monitoring, and performance analytics.

Our Vision

To create an intuitive, transparent, and accessible digital ecosystem that simplifies university workflows, eliminates administrative barriers, and enables students to focus on excellence.

Our Mission

Provide real-time visibility into academic records, attendance stats, upcoming assignments, and semester results through a secure, responsive, user-friendly interface.

Pillar 1: Transparency

Real-Time Academic Metrics

Students get immediate visibility into attendance criteria, internal assessments, and continuous evaluations without delay.

Pillar 2: Accessibility

Multi-Device Responsive Design

Designed to perform seamlessly on mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop workstations with light/dark theme support.

Pillar 3: Innovation

B.Tech CE Curriculum Excellence

Aligned with outcome-based education standards set by DEPSTAR and CHARUSAT University.

Built for Modern Academic Standards

Semantic HTML5

Structured accessible elements following W3C standards.

CSS Grid & Flexbox

Responsive multi-device layout adapting seamlessly across screens.

Modular Stylesheets & Vanilla JS

Clean individual CSS stylesheets and dynamic DOM JavaScript components.
